# C1 grammar batch 3: error-spot, matching and fill-in across topics
es | VT | The company [is] trading profitably since the new manager took over. | has been | "Since" with an ongoing situation needs the present perfect.
es | VT | At present, the committee [discusses] a number of proposals. | is discussing | The present continuous shows a temporary activity.
es | VT | By the time she retires, she [works] here for forty years. | will have worked | The future perfect shows total duration.
es | MOD | You [mustn't] have worried; the result was never in doubt. | needn't | "Needn't have" says the worry was unnecessary.
es | MOD | It is essential that he [is] informed at once. | be | The mandative subjunctive uses the base form.
es | MOD | He [might] has taken the wrong turning. | have | After "might" use "have".
es | MOD | The management insisted that she [attends] the meeting. | attend | After "insist that" the base form is used.
es | MOD | You [ought] be more careful when you cross the road. | ought to | "Ought" needs "to".
es | ART | I have [few] doubt that she will succeed. | little | "Doubt" is uncountable.
es | ART | There were [less] visitors than last year. | fewer | "Fewer" goes with countable nouns.
es | ART | He gave me [a] useful advice about the course. | some | "Advice" is uncountable.
es | ART | [Neither] of the three proposals was accepted. | None | "Neither" is for two.
es | ART | She has done [many] research on the subject. | much | "Research" is uncountable.
es | ART | All the [informations] have been checked. | information | "Information" is uncountable.
es | ART | A [large] amount of people attended the demonstration. | number | "Number" goes with countable nouns.
es | PREP | The firm is dependent [of] a single customer. | on | "Dependent on" is the fixed collocation.
es | PREP | He was accused [with] stealing the money. | of | "Accused of" introduces the alleged offence.
es | PREP | They congratulated her [for] the promotion. | on | "Congratulate on" is the standard collocation.
es | PREP | She is not accustomed [with] such treatment. | to | "Accustomed to" is the fixed collocation.
es | PREP | We are looking forward [for] meeting you. | to | "Look forward to" is the fixed phrase.
es | PREP | The company is committed [in] improving safety. | to | "Committed to" is the fixed collocation.
es | PREP | He took no notice [to] the warning. | of | "Take no notice of" is the fixed phrase.
es | PRON | The director, [whom] I believe is retiring, spoke briefly. | who | "Who" is the subject of "is retiring".
es | PRON | The house [which] I was born has been demolished. | where | "Where" follows a noun of place.
es | PRON | Those [which] have not registered should do so now. | who | "Who" refers to people.
es | PRON | The two sisters never speak to [themselves]. | each other | "Each other" is reciprocal.
es | PRON | It is [me] who is responsible for the error. | I | The formal subject form follows "it is".
es | PRON | The speaker, [who's] ideas were controversial, left early. | whose | "Whose" shows possession.
es | CONF | The new law will [effect] everyone in the country. | affect | "Affect" is the verb.
es | CONF | She is a very [sensible] child and cries easily. | sensitive | "Sensitive" means easily affected.
es | CONF | The [principle] of the school welcomed the visitors. | principal | "Principal" is the head of a school.
es | PHR | I can't put [up] this noise any longer. | up with | "Put up with" means to tolerate.
es | PHR | The project fell [down] because of lack of funds. | through | "Fall through" means to fail.
es | ADJ | The results were [considerable] better than expected. | considerably | An adverb is needed.
es | GER | I look forward [to] receive your reply. | receiving | "Look forward to" is followed by -ing.
es | T | The ferry [departed] to the island at noon. | for | "Depart for" introduces the destination.
es | W | The firm [employs] three hundred people since 2010. | has employed | "Since" calls for the present perfect.
es | S | There has been a sharp rise [of] unemployment. | in | "A rise in" is the fixed collocation.
mp | VT | Match each time expression with its tense. | by next week > future perfect ;; for the past year > present perfect continuous ;; before she left > past perfect ;; at this moment > present continuous | Time expressions.
mp | MOD | Match each modal structure with its meaning. | can't have > negative deduction about the past ;; might have > possibility about the past ;; ought to have > criticism of the past ;; needn't have > unnecessary action done | Past modals.
mp | ART | Match each phrase with its meaning. | hardly any > almost none ;; a handful > a very small number ;; a great deal > a very large amount ;; a good many > a large number ;; the odd > occasional | Quantifiers.
mp | PREP | Match each phrase with its meaning. | in view of > considering ;; on behalf of > representing ;; by means of > using ;; in lieu of > instead of ;; in the event of > if there should be | Prepositional phrases.
mp | PRON | Match each word with its use. | whomever > object form of whoever ;; whichever > a limited choice ;; whatever > any thing ;; wherever > any place ;; however > in any way | Wh-ever words.
mp | CONF | Match each pair with the correct use. | lie > recline ;; lay > put down ;; rise > go up ;; raise > lift ;; sit > be seated | Confusing verbs.
mp | PHR | Match each phrasal verb with its meaning. | bring up > raise a child or topic ;; carry out > perform ;; look into > investigate ;; turn down > refuse ;; come across > find by chance | Phrasal verbs.
mp | PASS | Match each active sentence with its passive. | They are building a bridge. > A bridge is being built. ;; They have built a bridge. > A bridge has been built. ;; They will build a bridge. > A bridge will be built. ;; They built a bridge. > A bridge was built. | Passive forms.

mp | ADJ | Match each adverb with the adjective it collocates with. | bitterly > cold ;; blissfully > unaware ;; painfully > obvious ;; wildly > exaggerated ;; fiercely > independent | Collocations.

mp | W | Match each word with its meaning. | tenure > the period of holding a position ;; remuneration > payment for work ;; accountability > responsibility for actions ;; delegate > assign a task to someone ;; incumbent > the current holder of a post | Work words.
mp | S | Match each word with its meaning. | mortality > the death rate ;; morbidity > the rate of illness ;; sanitation > systems for clean water and waste ;; epidemic > a widespread outbreak ;; endemic > regularly found in an area | Public health words.
