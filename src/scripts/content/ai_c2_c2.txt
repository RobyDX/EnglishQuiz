fb | MOD | I insist that she ___ present at the meeting. | be | is :: The mandative subjunctive uses the base form. | will be :: "Will" is not used here. | "Insist that" takes the base form.
fb | MOD | It is time we ___ a decision, don't you think? | made | make :: "It is time" takes a past form. | will make :: "Will" is not used here. | "It is time" is followed by the past simple.
