mc | E | I usually ___ my teeth before I go to bed. | brush | wash :: We "wash" hands and face, but we "brush" teeth. | clean up :: "Clean up" means to tidy a place, not teeth. | make :: "Make" is not used with teeth. | "Brush your teeth" is the standard collocation.
mc | E | Please ___ the dishes after dinner. | wash up | make :: You make a meal, not the dishes. | do up :: "Do up" means to fasten. | put on :: "Put on" is for clothes. | "Wash up" and "wash the dishes" are standard ways to clean plates after a meal.
