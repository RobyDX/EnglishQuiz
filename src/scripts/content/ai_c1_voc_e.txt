vf | E | She would have called you if she ___ your number. | had had | has :: The third conditional needs the past perfect. | have had :: "Have had" is not the past perfect. | would have :: "Would" is not used in the if-clause. | The if-clause uses the past perfect.
vf | E | It was not until I met her ___ I realised how much I had missed my family. | that | when :: "It was not until ... when" is informal. | which :: "Which" is ungrammatical here. | what :: "What" is ungrammatical here. | "It was not until ... that" is a cleft structure.
vf | E | I wish I ___ so impulsive when I was younger; I would have saved myself a lot of trouble. | hadn't been | wasn't :: A wish about the past needs the past perfect. | weren't :: This refers to the present. | haven't been :: The present perfect does not fit. | "Wish" + past perfect expresses regret.
vf | E | Little ___ they know that their surprise was about to be ruined. | did | do :: "Little" at the start requires inversion with the past. | have :: "Have" does not fit the past narrative. | were :: "Were they know" is ungrammatical. | "Little did they know" is an inverted structure.
