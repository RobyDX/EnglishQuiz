# B1 top-up 8
vc | T | The flight ___ (delay) for two hours because of the fog yesterday. | was delayed | delayed :: A flight cannot delay itself. | is delayed :: "Yesterday" needs the past. | has delayed :: A flight cannot delay. | "Was delayed" is the past passive.
fb | PREP | The tourists were warned ___ pickpockets in the crowded market. | about | of :: "Warned of" is possible but "about" is the usual combination here. | from :: "Warned from" is not correct. | at :: "Warned at" is not correct. | "Warn about" introduces the danger.
wb | PREP | The students were divided ___ four groups for the project. | into | in :: "Divided in" is less usual than "into". | to :: "Divided to" is not correct. | by :: "Divided by" is used in maths. | "Divided into" shows the result of dividing.
vf | E | I wish I ___ so much last night. I feel terrible. | hadn't eaten | didn't eat :: A past regret needs the past perfect. | wouldn't eat :: "Wouldn't" does not express a past regret. | haven't eaten :: The present perfect does not fit. | "Wish" + past perfect expresses regret.
vc | W | The new employee ___ (introduce) to the team on her first morning. | was introduced | introduced :: The employee is the receiver. | is introduced :: The sentence is in the past. | has introduced :: "has introduced" does not work here, because the employee is the receiver. | "Was introduced" is the past passive.
vc | S | If more trees ___ (plant) in the city, the air would be cleaner. | were planted | planted :: Trees cannot plant themselves. | are planted :: The second conditional needs a past form. | would be planted :: "Would" is not used in the if-clause. | "If trees were planted" is the unreal passive.
mc | W | Which sentence is correct? | She has worked here for ten years. | She works here for ten years. :: "For ten years" up to now needs a perfect form. | She worked here since ten years. :: "Since" is followed by a point in time. | She is working here since ten years. :: "She is working here since ten years." does not work here, because "Since" is followed by a point in time. | "For" with duration up to now uses the present perfect.
