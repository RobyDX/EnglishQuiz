vf | W | The management ___ the staff that no redundancies were planned. | assured | ensured :: "Ensure" does not take a personal object like this. | insured :: "Insure" is about insurance. | reassured :: "Reassured the staff" changes the structure. | "Assure someone that" means to promise.
vf | W | By the end of next year, the firm ___ its operations to three new countries. | will have expanded | expands :: A present tense does not fit. | is expanding :: The present continuous does not show completion. | had expanded :: The past perfect does not fit a future time. | "By the end of next year" calls for the future perfect.
vf | W | Had the board acted sooner, the company ___ in such difficulties now. | would not be | will not be :: "Will" does not fit the unreal condition. | would not have been :: The result refers to the present. | was not :: A past form does not fit the mixed conditional. | A mixed conditional links a past condition to a present result.
vf | W | The decision, ___ was taken without consultation, provoked a wave of protest. | which | what :: "What" cannot introduce a relative clause. | that :: "That" cannot follow a comma. | whose :: "Whose" shows possession. | "Which" introduces a nonrestrictive clause.
