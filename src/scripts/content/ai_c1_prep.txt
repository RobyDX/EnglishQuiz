# C1 prepositions and dependent prepositions
mc | PREP | The committee has been charged ___ drafting a code of conduct for all members. | with | for :: "Charged for" would mean being billed for. | of :: "Charged of" is not standard here. | to :: "Charged to" is not standard here. | "Charged with" introduces a task or an accusation.
mc | PREP | The firm's reputation rests ___ its commitment to quality rather than on advertising. | on | in :: "Rests in" is not the standard collocation. | at :: "Rests at" is not standard. | with :: "Rests with" suggests responsibility. | "Rest on" means to depend on.
mc | PREP | Several residents took exception ___ the council's decision to close the library. | to | at :: "Take exception at" is not standard. | with :: "Take exception with" is not standard. | for :: "Take exception for" is not standard. | "Take exception to" means to object to.
mc | PREP | He was dismissed ___ grounds of persistent absenteeism. | on | for :: "For grounds" is not standard. | by :: "By grounds" is not standard. | from :: "From grounds" is not standard. | "On grounds of" introduces the reason officially given.
mc | PREP | The two reports differ ___ their assessment of the risks involved. | in | on :: "Differ on" would introduce the topic of disagreement. | at :: "Differ at" is not standard. | by :: "Differ by" introduces an amount. | "Differ in" introduces the respect in which things are different.
mc | PREP | The two parties disagreed ___ how the funds should be allocated. | on | about :: "Disagree about" is possible, but "on" is the more usual with questions of principle. | at :: "Disagree at" is not standard. | for :: "Disagree for" is not standard. | "Disagree on" introduces the point of contention.
mc | PREP | She has a remarkable aptitude ___ languages and picks up new ones with ease. | for | in :: "Aptitude in" is rare. | of :: "Aptitude of" is not standard. | to :: "Aptitude to" is less common. | "Aptitude for" is the standard collocation.
mc | PREP | The minister was accused ___ misleading Parliament about the true cost of the scheme. | of | for :: "Accused for" is not standard. | with :: "Accused with" is not standard. | on :: "Accused on" is not standard. | "Accused of" introduces the alleged offence.
mc | PREP | The firm was fined ___ breaching environmental regulations. | for | of :: "Fined of" is not standard. | with :: "Fined with" is not standard. | at :: "Fined at" is not standard. | "Fined for" introduces the offence.
mc | PREP | We are indebted ___ our colleagues in the laboratory for their painstaking work. | to | for :: "Indebted for" would introduce the thing owed. | with :: "Indebted with" is not standard. | at :: "Indebted at" is not standard. | "Indebted to" introduces the person owed.
mc | PREP | The proposal met ___ fierce opposition from the unions. | with | by :: "Met by" is possible but "met with" suggests encountering a reaction. | to :: "Met to" is not standard. | in :: "Met in" is not standard. | "Meet with opposition" means to encounter it.
mc | PREP | It is advisable to take out insurance ___ theft before travelling. | against | for :: "Insurance for theft" is acceptable but "against" is the standard collocation. | of :: "Insurance of theft" is not standard. | on :: "Insurance on theft" is not standard. | "Insurance against" introduces the risk covered.
mc | PREP | The decision to merge was taken ___ the advice of the company's lawyers. | against | on :: "On the advice" would mean following it. | to :: "To the advice" is not standard. | by :: "By the advice" is not standard. | "Against the advice of" means ignoring it.
mc | PREP | The scheme has been designed ___ mind the needs of elderly residents. | with | in :: "In mind" lacks the preposition required before the noun phrase. | to :: "To mind" is not standard. | by :: "By mind" is not standard. | "With ... in mind" is a fixed structure.
mc | PREP | The company has been operating ___ a loss for the past three years. | at | in :: "In a loss" is not standard. | on :: "On a loss" is not standard. | with :: "With a loss" is not the fixed phrase. | "At a loss" is the standard collocation for business.
mc | PREP | We apologise for any inconvenience caused ___ the temporary closure of the library. | by | with :: "Caused with" is not standard. | from :: "Caused from" is not standard. | through :: "Caused through" is less usual. | "Caused by" introduces the agent or reason.
mc | PREP | The report is based ___ interviews with more than two hundred employees. | on | in :: "Based in" means located in. | at :: "Based at" means located at. | upon the :: The extra article breaks the phrase. | "Based on" shows the source.
mc | PREP | The inspector took no notice ___ the complaints of the residents. | of | to :: "Take notice to" is not standard. | for :: "Take notice for" is not standard. | at :: "Take notice at" is not standard. | "Take notice of" is the fixed phrase.
fb | PREP | There is a growing discrepancy ___ what politicians promise and what they deliver. | between | among :: "Among" is used with more than two items. | of :: "Discrepancy of" does not take two terms. | with :: "Discrepancy with" needs a different structure. | "Discrepancy between" compares two things.
fb | PREP | The new regulations are ___ accordance with European law. | in | on :: "On accordance" is not standard. | at :: "At accordance" is not standard. | by :: "By accordance" is not standard. | "In accordance with" is a fixed phrase.
fb | PREP | The project was completed ___ budget and ahead of schedule. | under | below :: "Below budget" is possible but "under budget" is the idiom. | beneath :: "Beneath budget" is not standard. | less :: "Less budget" is not standard. | "Under budget" means costing less than planned.
fb | PREP | He wrote the report ___ behalf of the entire department. | on | in :: "In behalf of" is American usage with a different meaning. | for :: "For behalf of" is not standard. | at :: "At behalf of" is not standard. | "On behalf of" means representing.
fb | PREP | ___ the face of mounting criticism, the minister refused to resign. | In | On :: "On the face of" has a different meaning. | At :: "At the face of" is not standard. | By :: "By the face of" is not standard. | "In the face of" means despite.
fb | PREP | She succeeded ___ the face of overwhelming odds. | in | at :: "Succeed at the face" is not standard. | on :: "Succeed on the face" is not standard. | by :: "Succeed by the face" is not standard. | "Succeed in the face of" means succeed despite.
fb | PREP | Contrary ___ popular belief, the Great Wall is not visible from space. | to | with :: "Contrary with" is not standard. | of :: "Contrary of" is not standard. | at :: "Contrary at" is not standard. | "Contrary to" is the fixed phrase.
fb | PREP | The law applies ___ all citizens without exception. | to | on :: "Applies on" is not standard. | for :: "Applies for" means to request. | at :: "Applies at" is not standard. | "Apply to" means to be relevant to.
fb | PREP | The negotiations were conducted ___ the strictest confidence. | in | with :: "With confidence" has a different meaning. | under :: "Under confidence" is not standard. | at :: "At confidence" is not standard. | "In confidence" means secretly.
fb | PREP | He is renowned ___ his generosity towards young researchers. | for | of :: "Renowned of" is not standard. | with :: "Renowned with" is not standard. | by :: "Renowned by" is not standard. | "Renowned for" introduces the reason for fame.
fb | PREP | She was ___ the impression that the contract had already been signed. | under | in :: "In the impression" is not standard. | on :: "On the impression" is not standard. | with :: "With the impression" is not standard. | "Under the impression" means believing.
fb | PREP | Prices have risen ___ an average of three per cent since January. | by | with :: "With an average" is not standard. | at :: "At an average" would describe a rate. | of :: "Of an average" is not standard. | "By" introduces the amount of change.
fb | PREP | The company is ___ the verge of bankruptcy after a series of bad investments. | on | at :: "At the verge" is not standard. | in :: "In the verge" is not standard. | by :: "By the verge" is not standard. | "On the verge of" is the fixed phrase.
fb | PREP | ___ no account should visitors be allowed into the laboratory unaccompanied. | On | At :: "At no account" is not standard. | In :: "In no account" is not standard. | By :: "By no account" is not standard. | "On no account" is a fixed negative phrase.
fb | PREP | I have nothing to say ___ the matter at this stage. | on | of :: "Say of the matter" changes the sense. | in :: "Say in the matter" is not standard. | by :: "Say by the matter" is not standard. | "Say on the matter" means to comment on the topic.
fb | PREP | They won the contract ___ the strength of their previous work. | on | by :: "By the strength" is not the usual phrase. | in :: "In the strength" is not standard. | with :: "With the strength" is not the idiom. | "On the strength of" means because of.
vf | PREP | The scheme has been criticised ___ the grounds that it benefits only the wealthy. | on | at :: "Criticised at the grounds" is not standard. | by :: "By the grounds" is not standard. | for :: "For the grounds" is not standard. | "On the grounds that" introduces a reason.
vf | PREP | The company is committed ___ reducing its carbon emissions by half. | to | for :: "Committed for" is not standard. | with :: "Committed with" is not standard. | in :: "Committed in" is not standard. | "Committed to" is followed by -ing.
vf | PREP | We look forward ___ hearing from you at your earliest convenience. | to | for :: "Look forward for" is not standard. | at :: "Look forward at" is not standard. | on :: "Look forward on" is not standard. | "Look forward to" is followed by -ing.
vf | PREP | The success of the venture depends largely ___ the goodwill of local people. | on | of :: "Depend of" is not standard. | from :: "Depend from" is not standard. | at :: "Depend at" is not standard. | "Depend on" is the fixed collocation.
vf | PREP | She was reprimanded ___ failing to report the incident promptly. | for | of :: "Reprimanded of" is not standard. | on :: "Reprimanded on" is not standard. | with :: "Reprimanded with" is not standard. | "Reprimanded for" introduces the reason.
vf | PREP | The government is bound ___ honour the terms of the treaty. | to | by :: "Bound by honour" would change the structure. | for :: "Bound for" means heading towards. | at :: "Bound at" is not standard. | "Bound to" is followed by an infinitive.
vf | PREP | The lecturer was greeted ___ a round of applause as he entered the hall. | with | by :: "Greeted by" would introduce the person who greeted him. | at :: "Greeted at" is not standard. | to :: "Greeted to" is not standard. | "Greeted with" introduces the reaction.
vf | PREP | They are determined to stand ___ their principles whatever the cost. | by | for :: "Stand for" means to represent. | on :: "Stand on" is not the idiom. | at :: "Stand at" is not the idiom. | "Stand by" means to remain loyal to.
vc | PREP | The board is reluctant to commit itself ___ (to / spend) such a large sum. | to spending | to spend :: After "commit oneself to" use -ing. | for spending :: "Commit for" is not standard. | in spending :: "Commit in" is not standard. | "Commit to" is followed by -ing.
vc | PREP | He was suspected ___ (of / leak) confidential information to the press. | of leaking | for leaking :: "Suspected for" is not standard. | to leak :: "Suspected to" changes the structure. | of leak :: After a preposition use -ing. | "Suspected of" is followed by -ing.
vc | PREP | She has no objection ___ (to / work) at weekends. | to working | for working :: "Objection for" is not standard. | of working :: "Objection of" is not standard. | to work :: After "objection to" use -ing. | "Objection to" is followed by -ing.
vc | PREP | We are in the process ___ (of / negotiate) a new contract with the supplier. | of negotiating | for negotiating :: "Process for" changes the sense. | to negotiate :: "Process to" is not standard here. | of negotiate :: After a preposition use -ing. | "In the process of" is followed by -ing.
