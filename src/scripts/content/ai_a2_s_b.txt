# A2 society, health, environment: sentence-choice (typical learner mistakes), word-order, odd-one-out
sc | S+ART | I have a headache and a sore throat. | I have a head pain and a sore throat. :: We say "headache", not "head pain". | I have headache and a sore throat. :: "Headache" is countable here, so we need "a": a headache. | We say a headache and a sore throat.
sc | S | The doctor gave me a prescription for the medicine. | The doctor gave me a recipe for the medicine. :: A recipe tells you how to cook a dish. A paper for medicine is a prescription. | The doctor gave me a receipt for the medicine. :: A receipt shows what you paid for something. | The doctor writes a prescription for medicine.
sc | S+ADJ | She is in good health. | She is in good healthy. :: "healthy" is an adjective. After "good" we need the noun "health". | She is in good illness. :: "illness" means being ill, the opposite idea. | Good health is the noun for being well.
sc | S+ADJ | I feel ill, so I am staying in bed. | I feel illness, so I am staying in bed. :: "illness" is a noun. After "feel" we need an adjective: ill. | I feel sickness, so I am staying in bed. :: "sickness" is a noun. After "feel" we need an adjective: sick or ill. | After "feel" we use an adjective: ill.
sc | S+ART | There is a lot of traffic in the city. | There are a lot of traffic in the city. :: "traffic" is uncountable, so we use "is". | There is a lot of traffics in the city. :: "traffic" has no plural form. | Uncountable nouns take a singular verb.
sc | S | The weather is bad today. | The weather are bad today. :: "weather" is singular, so we use "is". | The time is bad today. :: We talk about rain, sun and wind as "weather", not "time". | Weather is uncountable and singular.
sc | S+PREP | We must protect the environment from pollution. | We must protect the environment for pollution. :: We say "protect from" something bad, not "protect for". | We must protect the environment of pollution. :: "protect of" is wrong: we say "protect from". | We protect something from danger.
sc | S+PRON | Many people recycle glass and paper. | Many peoples recycle glass and paper. :: "people" is already plural. | Many people recycles glass and paper. :: "people" is plural, so the verb is "recycle". | "People" takes a plural verb.
sc | S+ADJ | The Earth is getting warmer. | The Earth is getting more warm. :: With short adjectives we add -er: warmer. | The Earth is getting warming. :: After "getting" we use an adjective: warmer. | The comparative of "warm" is "warmer".
sc | S+VT | It is raining now, so take an umbrella. | It rains now, so take an umbrella. :: For an action happening now we use the present continuous: is raining. | It is rain now, so take an umbrella. :: "rain" is a noun. The verb form is "is raining". | We use is/are + -ing for what is happening now.
sc | S+ART | There are a lot of mountains in my country. | There is a lot of mountains in my country. :: "mountains" is plural, so we use "are". | There are a lot of mountain in my country. :: After "a lot of" a plural noun needs -s: mountains. | Plural nouns take "are".
sc | S+ADJ | He was ill last week, but now he is better. | He was ill last week, but now he is gooder. :: The comparative of "good" is "better". | He were ill last week, but now he is better. :: With "he" we use "was", not "were". | "Better" is the comparative of "good".
sc | S+PREP | I am afraid of spiders. | I am afraid from spiders. :: We say "afraid of", not "afraid from". | I am afraid with spiders. :: We say "afraid of", not "afraid with". | The correct phrase is "afraid of".
sc | S+CONF | How often do you do exercise? | How often do you make exercise? :: We say "do exercise", not "make exercise". | How often do you play exercise? :: We say "do exercise". We play games and sports. | Exercise is something we do.
sc | S+ART | He has a lot of energy. | He has a lot of energies. :: In this meaning "energy" is uncountable and has no plural. | He has much of energy. :: We say "a lot of energy" or "much energy", not "much of energy". | Use "a lot of" with uncountable nouns in positive sentences.
sc | S+GER | Wear sun cream to avoid sunburn. | Wear sun cream for avoid sunburn. :: To say why we do something we use "to + base verb", not "for + verb". | Wear sun cream to avoiding sunburn. :: After "to" we use the base form: avoid. | We use "to + verb" for purpose.
sc | S+MOD | The patient must stay in bed for three days. | The patient must to stay in bed for three days. :: After "must" we use the base form without "to". | The patient must stays in bed for three days. :: After "must" we never add -s. | After "must" use the base verb.
sc | S+MOD | You should drink more water. | You should to drink more water. :: After "should" we use the base form without "to". | You should drinking more water. :: After "should" we use the base form, not -ing. | After "should" use the base verb.
sc | S+ART | Take two tablets after meals. | Take two tablet after meals. :: After "two" we need a plural noun: tablets. | Take two tablets after a meals. :: "meals" is plural and cannot take "a". | After a number above one use a plural noun.
sc | S+GER | Don't forget to wash your hands. | Don't forget washing your hands. :: "forget to + verb" means you must do it. "forget washing" means you do not remember a past action. | Don't forget wash your hands. :: After "forget" we need "to + verb". | Use "forget to" for a task you must do.
sc | S+PREP | She is allergic to nuts. | She is allergic of nuts. :: We say "allergic to", not "allergic of". | She is allergic with nuts. :: We say "allergic to", not "allergic with". | The correct phrase is "allergic to".
sc | S+PRON | My eyes hurt when I read for a long time. | My eyes hurts when I read for a long time. :: "eyes" is plural, so the verb has no -s. | My eye hurt when I read for a long time. :: We have two eyes, so the plural is "eyes". | Plural subjects take a plural verb.
sc | S+PREP | The forest is full of animals. | The forest is full from animals. :: We say "full of", not "full from". | The forest is full with animals. :: We say "full of", not "full with". | The correct phrase is "full of".
sc | S+VT | The volunteers cleaned the beach last Sunday. | The volunteers cleaned the beach next Sunday. :: "cleaned" is the past, so it needs "last", not "next". | The volunteers clean the beach last Sunday. :: "last Sunday" is in the past, so we need the past simple: cleaned. | Use the past simple with "last Sunday".
sc | S+VT | I never saw a dolphin before this holiday. | I never see a dolphin before this holiday. :: "before this holiday" talks about the past, so we need the past simple: saw. | I never seen a dolphin before this holiday. :: "seen" needs "have". The simple past of "see" is "saw". | Use "saw" for a finished past event.
sc | S+ART | We need some information about the weather. | We need some informations about the weather. :: "information" is uncountable and has no plural. | We need an information about the weather. :: We cannot use "an" with an uncountable noun. | Use "some information" or "a piece of information".
