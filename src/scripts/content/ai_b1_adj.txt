# B1 adjectives and adverbs

mc | ADJ | The weather today is ___ than yesterday. | worse | bad :: "Bad" is the basic form, but a comparison is needed. | badder :: "Bad" has an irregular comparative. | worst :: "Worst" is the superlative. | The comparative of "bad" is "worse".
mc | ADJ | This is the ___ film I have ever seen. | best | better :: "Better" compares only two things. | good :: "Good" is the base form. | most good :: "Good" has an irregular superlative. | The superlative of "good" is "best".
mc | ADJ | My new phone is not as ___ as my old one. | reliable | more reliable :: After "as ... as" we use the base adjective. | reliably :: After "be" we need an adjective, not an adverb. | reliability :: A noun does not fit after "as ... as". | "As ... as" is used with the base form.

mc | ADJ | It's ___ cold to go swimming today. | too | enough :: "Enough" goes after the adjective. | very :: "Very" does not express a problem. | so :: "So" does not express a problem here. | "Too" means more than is acceptable.
mc | ADJ | The water isn't warm ___ to swim in. | enough | too :: "Too" goes before the adjective. | very :: "Very" goes before the adjective. | much :: "Much" is not used after an adjective. | "Enough" goes after the adjective.
mc | ADJ | It was ___ a good book that I read it twice. | such | so :: "So" is followed directly by an adjective. | too :: "Too" does not express a result. | very :: "Very" does not express a result. | "Such a" + adjective + noun expresses a result.
mc | ADJ | The book was ___ interesting that I couldn't put it down. | so | such :: "Such" is followed by a noun phrase. | too :: "Too" does not express a result. | very :: "Very" does not express a result. | "So" + adjective expresses a result.
