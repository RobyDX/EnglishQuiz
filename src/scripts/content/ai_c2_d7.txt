# C2 batch d7 (mc fb vocabulary and collocations)
mc | T | The route ___ through spectacular gorges before reaching the plateau. | winds | wounds :: "Wounds" means injuries. | winces :: "Winces" means flinches. | "Winds" means follows a curving path.
mc | T | Tourists are advised to ___ the midday sun in the tropics. | shun | shin :: A "shin" is part of the leg. | shout :: "Shout" means call loudly. | "Shun" means avoid.
mc | T | The mountain hut was basic but ___ , with a roaring fire and hot soup. | welcoming | welcome :: "Welcome" does not follow "but" naturally here. | welcomed :: "Welcomed" is a participle. | "Welcoming" means friendly.
mc | E | His ___ nature made him popular among his students. | genial | general :: "General" means common. | genius :: "Genius" is exceptional ability. | "Genial" means friendly.
mc | E | She hid her disappointment behind a ___ smile. | brave | brace :: A "brace" is a support. | brake :: A "brake" is a stopping device. | A "brave smile" hides distress.
mc | E | It was a ___ remark, and he regretted it as soon as he had said it. | careless | careful :: "Careful" has the opposite sense. | carefree :: "Carefree" means without worries. | "Careless" means thoughtless.
mc | E | He is not given to ___ , and rarely praises his own achievements. | boasting | boarding :: "Boarding" means getting on. | bathing :: "Bathing" means washing. | "Boasting" means bragging.
mc | E | She greeted the news with a ___ smile, revealing nothing of her feelings. | tight | tide :: A "tide" is the sea's rise and fall. | tile :: A "tile" is a flat piece. | "A tight smile" is a strained one.
mc | W | The proposed merger has ___ opposition from the unions. | provoked | provided :: "Provided" means supplied. | proved :: "Proved" means demonstrated. | "Provoked" means caused.
mc | W | Staff were told to ___ any complaints in writing. | submit | subdue :: "Subdue" means overcome. | subsist :: "Subsist" means survive. | "Submit" means hand in.
mc | W | The manager's remark was meant as a ___ , but nobody laughed. | joke | jock :: A "jock" is an athlete. | joker :: A "joker" is a person. | A "joke" is a funny remark.
mc | W | The firm's success is largely ___ to the hard work of its staff. | attributable | attributed :: "Attributed" is a participle. | attribution :: "Attribution" is a noun. | "Attributable to" means due to.
mc | S | The scientist's findings were ___ by a rival team. | refuted | refunded :: "Refunded" means paid back. | refused :: "Refused" means declined. | "Refuted" means proved wrong.
mc | S | Experts say the epidemic is ___ in the north but still spreading in the south. | abating | abetting :: "Abetting" means helping a crime. | abasing :: "Abasing" means humbling. | "Abating" means decreasing.
mc | S | The outbreak was ___ within weeks by prompt action. | contained | containing :: An auxiliary is missing. | contend :: "Contend" means compete. | "Contained" means kept under control.
fb | T | The hotel is ___ in a quiet side street, away from the bustle of the centre. | tucked | tacked :: "Tacked" means attached. | ticked :: "Ticked" means marked. | "Tucked away" means hidden.
fb | T | Visitors are ___ to taste the local wines at the cellar door. | invited | invading :: "Invading" means attacking. | inviting :: A passive participle is needed. | "Invited to" is a passive.
fb | T | We were lucky to find a table in the ___ restaurant overlooking the bay. | packed | pecked :: "Pecked" is a bird action. | pocked :: "Pocked" means marked. | "Packed" means very full.
fb | E | He made no ___ to hide his irritation. | attempt | attempted :: "Attempted" is a participle. | attention :: "Attention" is a noun. | "Make no attempt" is the standard collocation.
fb | E | After years of neglect, the friendship was beyond ___ . | repair | repay :: "Repay" means pay back. | repeal :: "Repeal" means cancel. | "Beyond repair" means too damaged to fix.
fb | E | She told the story with such ___ that the whole table was laughing. | gusto | gust :: A "gust" is a puff of wind. | gutter :: A "gutter" is a channel. | "Gusto" means enthusiasm.
fb | W | The government has been accused of ___ the figures to disguise the true level of unemployment. | massaging | massacring :: "Massacring" means killing. | messaging :: "Messaging" means sending texts. | "Massaging" figures means manipulating them.
fb | W | The negotiators reached a ___ agreement that satisfied neither side fully. | compromise | comprise :: "Comprise" means consist of. | compress :: "Compress" means squeeze. | A "compromise agreement" involves concessions.
fb | W | She has built up a ___ reputation as an honest dealer. | sound | sand :: "Sand" is a gritty material. | sold :: "Sold" is a past form. | "A sound reputation" means a reliable one.
fb | W | The scheme was abandoned for lack of ___ . | funds | fund :: The plural is used in this phrase. | fond :: "Fond" means affectionate. | "For lack of funds" is the fixed phrase.
fb | W | All employees are ___ to the same code of conduct. | subject | subjected :: "Subjected" is a participle. | subjective :: "Subjective" means personal. | "Subject to" means bound by.
fb | S | The government has ___ a series of measures to tackle obesity. | unveiled | unwrapped :: "Unwrapped" means took the cover off. | unfolded :: "Unfolded" means opened out. | "Unveiled" means announced publicly.
fb | S | The disease is ___ by the bite of an infected mosquito. | transmitted | transmuted :: "Transmuted" means changed in form. | transposed :: "Transposed" means swapped. | "Transmitted" means passed on.
fb | S | The new drug has shown ___ promise in early trials. | considerable | considerate :: "Considerate" means thoughtful. | considering :: "Considering" is a participle. | "Considerable promise" means great promise.
fb | S | Pollution levels in the city are well above the ___ limit. | legal | legion :: A "legion" is a large group. | legible :: "Legible" means readable. | "Legal limit" is the permitted maximum.
fb | S | The charity relies on ___ from the public to fund its work. | donations | donors :: "Donors" are the givers. | donkeys :: "Donkeys" are animals. | "Donations" are gifts of money.
mc | PRON | I could not tell ___ of the twins was which. | which | what :: "What" does not suit a limited choice. | whom :: "Whom" cannot be the subject. | "Which of the twins" suits a limited choice.
mc | MOD | It is vital that every precaution ___ taken. | be | is :: The mandative subjunctive uses the base form. | will be :: "Will" is not used here. | "Vital that" takes the base form.
mc | VT | Hardly ___ the ship left port when the storm struck. | had | has :: The past perfect is needed. | did :: "Hardly did the ship left" is ungrammatical. | "Hardly had" is the standard inversion.
mc | PASS | He is believed to ___ in hiding since the scandal. | have been | has been :: "To" takes the base form. | being :: A participle does not follow "to". | The perfect infinitive shows a state up to now.
