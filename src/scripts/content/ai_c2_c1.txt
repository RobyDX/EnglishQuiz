# C2 batch c1: fb and mc vocabulary and collocations
fb | E | She received the news with ___ composure, to everyone's surprise. | remarkable | remark :: "Remark" is a noun or verb. | remarked :: "Remarked" is a participle. | "Remarkable composure" is a standard collocation.
fb | E | The children were ___ with excitement on the morning of the trip. | bubbling | bumbling :: "Bumbling" means clumsy. | brimming :: "Brimming" is possible but less natural. | "Bubbling with excitement" is a standard phrase.
fb | E | The old man's ___ eyes belied his age. | twinkling | twisting :: "Twisting" means turning. | twitching :: "Twitching" means moving jerkily. | "Twinkling eyes" suggests liveliness.
fb | E | They quickly became ___ , sharing every secret. | inseparable | inseparate :: This word does not exist. | insensible :: "Insensible" means unconscious. | "Inseparable" means always together.
fb | E | The long walk left her utterly ___ . | exhausted | exhausting :: The -ing form describes the cause. | exhaust :: "Exhaust" is a verb or noun. | "Exhausted" describes how she felt.
fb | E | He's a rather ___ sort of person, who never says what he thinks. | guarded | guard :: "Guard" is a noun or verb. | guardian :: A "guardian" is a protector. | "Guarded" means cautious in speech.
fb | E | The speaker had a ___ manner that put the audience at ease. | disarming | disarmed :: "Disarmed" is a participle. | disarray :: "Disarray" is disorder. | "Disarming" means winning over.
fb | T | The hotel, set in ___ grounds, was once a hunting lodge. | extensive | extended :: "Extended" is a participle. | extension :: "Extension" is a noun. | "Extensive grounds" means large grounds.
fb | W | Management insisted that redundancies were a last ___ . | resort | resource :: A "resource" is a supply. | resolve :: "Resolve" is determination. | "A last resort" is a fixed phrase.
fb | W | The firm has been ___ by the departure of several senior staff. | weakened | widened :: "Widened" means made broader. | wakened :: "Wakened" means woken. | "Weakened" means made less strong.
fb | W | The merger created a ___ with a global reach. | conglomerate | conglomeration :: "Conglomeration" is the process. | congregate :: "Congregate" is a verb. | A "conglomerate" is a large corporation.
fb | W | The consultants have been brought in to ___ the firm's finances. | overhaul | overlook :: "Overlook" means fail to notice. | overtake :: "Overtake" means pass. | "Overhaul" means thoroughly review.
fb | S | The research team has made a significant ___ in the treatment of malaria. | advance | advent :: The "advent" is arrival. | advantage :: An "advantage" is a benefit. | "Advance" means progress.
fb | S | Researchers have found a ___ between diet and heart disease. | link | lick :: "Lick" is to touch with the tongue. | lock :: "Lock" is a fastening. | A "link" is a connection.
mc | E | His behaviour was quite out of ___ for such a mild-mannered man. | character | characters :: The idiom uses the singular. | characteristic :: "Out of characteristic" is not the idiom. | "Out of character" means unlike a person's usual self.
mc | E | She put a brave ___ on it, but we could see she was hurt. | face | mask :: "Put a mask on it" is not the idiom. | smile :: "Put a smile on it" is not the idiom. | "Put a brave face on" is the idiom.
mc | E | He is not ___ to losing his temper, so his outburst surprised us. | given | giving :: "Giving to" is not standard. | gave :: "Gave to" is not standard. | "Not given to" means not inclined to.
mc | E | The news came as a bolt from the ___ . | blue | blur :: "Blur" is not the idiom. | blow :: "Blow" is not the idiom. | "A bolt from the blue" means a complete surprise.
mc | T | We took the scenic route, ___ the motorway altogether. | bypassing | bypass :: "Bypass" is a noun or verb. | bypassed :: "Bypassed" is a participle. | "Bypassing" is a participle phrase.
mc | W | The proposal was ___ with scepticism by the board. | received | perceived :: "Perceived" means noticed. | recited :: "Recited" means repeated aloud. | "Received with" means greeted with.
mc | W | Employees were asked to ___ any concerns directly to the HR department. | refer | defer :: "Defer" means postpone. | infer :: "Infer" means conclude. | "Refer" means send.
mc | W | The decision was made on the ___ of incomplete information. | basis | bases :: "Bases" is the plural. | basic :: "Basic" is an adjective. | "On the basis of" is the fixed phrase.
mc | S | The study ___ that there is no link between the two. | concludes | concluded :: The sentence states a current finding. | concluding :: An auxiliary is missing. | The present simple reports what a study concludes.
mc | S | The new technique has ___ the way surgery is performed. | revolutionised | revolved :: "Revolved" means turned. | revoked :: "Revoked" means cancelled. | "Revolutionised" means completely changed.
mc | S | Public ___ over the new law has been mixed. | opinion | opinions :: "Public opinion" is uncountable. | opinionated :: "Opinionated" means stubborn. | "Public opinion" is a fixed phrase.
mc | VT | We ___ about leaving when the taxi finally arrived. | were just | had just :: "Had just about leaving" is ungrammatical. | are just :: The sentence is in the past. | "Were just about to leave" means on the point of.
mc | MOD | Had they known the danger, they ___ never have gone. | would | will :: "Will" does not fit an unreal past. | should to :: "Should to" is ungrammatical. | "Would never have gone" completes the conditional.
mc | PREP | He was disappointed ___ the result of the vote. | with | in :: "Disappointed in" is possible but changes the sense. | of :: "Disappointed of" is not standard. | "Disappointed with" introduces the thing.
mc | ADJ | The ___ view from the summit made the climb worthwhile. | breathtaking | breathless :: "Breathless" means out of breath. | breathy :: "Breathy" describes a voice. | "Breathtaking" means astonishingly beautiful.
es | E | He was [boring] by the long speech. | bored | The -ed form describes how he felt.
es | W | They discussed [about] the budget for hours. | discussed | "Discuss" takes no preposition.
es | W | The firm is famous [by] its quality. | for | "Famous for" introduces the reason.
es | S | The disease is [spreaded] by mosquitoes. | spread | The past participle of "spread" is "spread".
es | S | There are [much] reasons to doubt the findings. | many | "Reasons" is countable.
ec | E | He was very surprising by her reply. | He was very surprised by her reply. | The -ed form describes how he felt. | Participial adjectives.
ec | T | The flight arrived with two hours delay. | The flight arrived two hours late. | "Late" is the natural expression. | Collocations.
ec | W | He explained me the details of the contract. | He explained the details of the contract to me. | "Explain" takes "to" before the person. | Verb patterns.
