# C1 verb tenses and aspect
mc | VT | I ___ you were going to apply for the job; nobody told me you had changed your mind. | assumed | have assumed :: The present perfect does not fit a past misunderstanding. | was assuming :: The continuous does not report a conclusion reached. | had assumed :: The past perfect needs a later past reference. | The past simple reports a conclusion reached at the time.
mc | VT | The more the minister tried to explain, the less convinced people ___. | became | had become :: The past perfect would imply an earlier completed change. | have become :: The present perfect does not fit past events. | were becoming :: The continuous weakens the parallel with "tried". | The past simple matches the correlative structure.
mc | VT | She ___ her degree for only a few months when she realised she had chosen the wrong subject. | had been pursuing | was pursuing :: The past perfect continuous shows duration before the realisation. | has been pursuing :: The present perfect does not fit past events. | pursued :: The simple past does not show duration. | The past perfect continuous shows duration up to a past moment.
mc | VT | I ___ about it all day and still cannot decide what to do. | have been thinking | think :: The present simple does not show duration. | am thinking :: The present continuous does not show duration up to now. | thought :: The simple past does not connect to the present. | The present perfect continuous links duration to the present.
mc | VT | At this time next week, I ___ on a beach in Thailand without a care in the world. | will be lying | will lie :: The future simple does not show an action in progress. | lie :: A present tense does not fit. | am lying :: The present continuous does not point to the future. | The future continuous shows an action in progress at a future time.
mc | VT | No sooner ___ the meeting started than the fire alarm went off. | had | has :: The past perfect is needed in the inverted clause. | did :: "No sooner did" is followed by the base form. | was :: "Was the meeting started" is passive. | "No sooner had" + subject + participle is the standard inversion.
mc | VT | Scarcely ___ she finished speaking when the audience burst into applause. | had | has :: The past perfect is needed. | did :: "Scarcely did she finished" is ungrammatical. | was :: "Was she finished" is passive. | "Scarcely had ... when" is the standard correlative pair.
mc | VT | It is the third time this month that the train ___ late. | has been | was :: "The third time" takes the present perfect. | is :: A present simple does not fit "this month". | had been :: The past perfect needs a past reference. | "It is the third time" takes the present perfect.
mc | VT | By the end of the decade, the population of the city ___ by almost fifty per cent. | will have grown | will grow :: The future perfect shows completion before a future time. | grows :: A present tense does not fit. | has grown :: The present perfect does not fit a future time. | "By the end of" calls for the future perfect.
mc | VT | I ___ for a taxi for twenty minutes before I realised that the service had been suspended. | had been waiting | was waiting :: The past perfect continuous shows duration before the realisation. | have been waiting :: The present perfect does not fit past events. | waited :: The simple past does not show duration. | The past perfect continuous shows duration before a past moment.
mc | VT | He claims that he ___ the money to charity before the investigation began. | had donated | donated :: The past perfect shows an earlier action. | has donated :: The present perfect does not fit. | was donating :: The continuous does not show completion. | The past perfect shows an action before another past event.
mc | VT | Hardly ___ the contract been signed when the first problems emerged. | had | has :: The past perfect is needed. | did :: "Did the contract been" is ungrammatical. | was :: "Was the contract been" is ungrammatical. | "Hardly had ... when" is the standard correlative pair.
mc | VT | I'm sure you ___ the new proposal by the time we meet tomorrow. | will have read | will read :: The future perfect shows completion before a future time. | read :: A past form does not fit. | are reading :: The present continuous does not show completion. | "By the time" + future perfect shows completion.
mc | VT | We ___ to sell the house, but in the end we decided to stay. | were going | went :: The past continuous with "going to" expresses an unrealised plan. | have gone :: The present perfect does not fit. | had gone :: The past perfect would imply the plan was completed. | "Was going to" expresses an unfulfilled intention.
mc | VT | The prime minister ___ to announce an election, but she has since changed her mind. | was due | had due :: "Had due" is ungrammatical. | is due :: The present does not show that the plan has changed. | has been due :: "Has been due" is not used with a changed plan. | "Was due to" refers to a scheduled event that did not happen.
mc | VT | Everyone ___ to leave when the announcement was made that the delayed flight would depart shortly. | was preparing | prepared :: The past continuous shows an action in progress. | has prepared :: The present perfect does not fit. | prepares :: The sentence is in the past. | The past continuous shows an action in progress at a past moment.
mc | VT | Although the firm ___ profitable for years, it has recently run into difficulties. | had been | has been :: The past perfect would mark an earlier period. | was :: The simple past does not link to the present. | is :: A present tense does not fit the concession. | The past perfect marks a state before the recent difficulties.
mc | VT | If only I ___ to her advice at the time; I wouldn't be in this mess now. | had listened | listened :: A mixed regret about the past needs the past perfect. | listen :: A present tense does not fit. | would listen :: "Would" does not express past regret. | "If only" + past perfect expresses a regret about the past.
fb | VT | The negotiators ___ since early morning, and there is still no sign of agreement. | have been talking | talk :: The present simple does not show duration. | are talking :: The present continuous does not show duration. | talked :: The simple past does not connect to now. | The present perfect continuous links duration to now.
fb | VT | When I arrived, they ___ the final paragraph of the report. | were still writing | still wrote :: The past continuous shows an action in progress. | had still written :: The past perfect would show completion. | have still written :: The present perfect does not fit. | The past continuous shows an action in progress.
fb | VT | At the moment, the government ___ a major reform of the pension system. | is considering | considers :: The present simple would describe a habit. | has considered :: The present perfect would imply completion. | considered :: The simple past does not fit "at the moment". | The present continuous shows a temporary activity.
fb | VT | By the time the rescue team arrived, the climbers ___ for nearly two days. | had been trapped | were trapped :: The past perfect shows an earlier state. | have been trapped :: The present perfect does not fit. | trapped :: The climbers are the receivers. | The past perfect passive shows a state before the arrival.
fb | VT | She ___ her keys somewhere in the house, but she cannot remember where. | must have left | must leave :: A deduction about the past needs a perfect form. | must be leaving :: The continuous does not fit. | might leave :: A present form does not fit. | "Must have left" is a deduction about the past.
fb | VT | Next month, he ___ the post of finance director for a full decade. | will have held | will hold :: The future perfect shows the completed period. | holds :: A present tense does not fit. | has held :: The present perfect does not fit "next month". | The future perfect marks the point at which a period is complete.
vf | VT | The company ___ steadily since the new management took over. | has been improving | is improving :: The present continuous does not show duration since a point. | improved :: The simple past does not connect to now. | improves :: The present simple does not show change. | The present perfect continuous links change to now.
vf | VT | By the time we got to the cinema, the film ___. | had already begun | already began :: The past perfect shows an earlier action. | has already begun :: The present perfect does not fit. | was already beginning :: The continuous does not show completion. | The past perfect shows an earlier action.
vf | VT | This time tomorrow we ___ our final exam. | will be sitting | will sit :: The future continuous shows an action in progress. | sit :: A present tense does not fit. | are sitting :: The present continuous does not point to the future. | The future continuous shows an action in progress.
vf | VT | I ___ about the matter until you mentioned it just now. | had not thought | did not think :: The past perfect shows an earlier state. | have not thought :: The present perfect does not fit "until just now". | was not thinking :: The continuous does not fit. | The past perfect marks the state before a past moment.
vf | VT | No sooner ___ she left the room than the arguing started again. | had | has :: The past perfect is needed. | did :: "Did she left" is ungrammatical. | was :: "Was she left" is passive. | "No sooner had" is the standard inversion.
vc | VT | By next June she ___ (study) medicine for exactly five years. | will have been studying | will study :: The future perfect continuous shows duration. | studies :: A present tense does not fit. | has studied :: The present perfect does not fit a future point. | The future perfect continuous shows duration up to a future moment.
vc | VT | I ___ (not / realise) how serious the situation was until I saw the pictures. | hadn't realised | didn't realise :: The past perfect marks the earlier state. | haven't realised :: The present perfect does not fit. | wasn't realising :: "Realise" is not used in the continuous. | The past perfect marks a state before a past moment.
vc | VT | The students ___ (revise) all week and are now ready for the exam. | have been revising | revised :: The present perfect continuous links duration to now. | revise :: The present simple does not show duration. | are revising :: The present continuous does not show duration. | The present perfect continuous links duration to now.
vc | VT | He ___ (work) on the novel for years when he finally abandoned it. | had been working | was working :: The past perfect continuous shows duration before the abandonment. | has been working :: The present perfect does not fit. | worked :: The simple past does not show duration. | The past perfect continuous shows duration before a past event.
vc | VT | If she ___ (know) about the delay, she would have left earlier. | had known | knew :: The third conditional needs the past perfect. | would know :: "Would" is not used in the if-clause. | has known :: The present perfect does not fit. | The if-clause uses the past perfect.
