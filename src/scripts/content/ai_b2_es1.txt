# B2 error-spot / place-word / error-correct / transform / word-order / match
es | VT | I [am] knowing the answer to that question. | know | "Know" is a stative verb and is not used in continuous forms.
es | VT | They [are] living here since 2015. | have been | "Since" with an ongoing situation needs the present perfect.
es | VT | By the time we arrived, the film [already] started. | had | A past perfect is needed for an earlier action.
es | VT | I [will] call you as soon as I will arrive. | arrive | Time clauses use the present simple for the future.
es | VT | He said he [will] come to the party tomorrow. | would | Reported speech shifts "will" to "would".
es | VT | When I was young, I [am] going to the sea every summer. | went | The story is in the past.
es | VT | She [didn't] used to like spicy food. | didn't use | After "didn't" use "use to".
es | VT | We [have] seen that film last week. | saw | "Last week" calls for the past simple.
es | VT | If I [will] see him, I'll tell him. | see | The if-clause uses the present simple.
es | GER | He is looking forward [to] meet you. | meeting | "Look forward to" is followed by -ing.
es | GER | They decided [staying] at home. | to stay | "Decide" is followed by an infinitive.
es | GER | I'm used [to] get up early. | getting | "Be used to" is followed by -ing.
es | GER | He refused [helping] us. | to help | "Refuse" is followed by an infinitive.
es | COND | If I [would] have more time, I would learn Chinese. | had | "Would" is not used in the if-clause.
es | COND | If she [studied] harder, she would have passed. | had studied | The third conditional needs the past perfect.
es | COND | I wish I [can] speak Spanish. | could | A wish about the present needs a past form.
es | COND | Unless you [don't] hurry, you will miss the train. | hurry | "Unless" already has a negative meaning.
es | COND | If I [was] you, I would apologise. | were | "If I were you" is the standard form.
es | COND | I'd rather you [don't] smoke here. | didn't | After "I'd rather" + person use a past form.
es | PASS | The house [is] being built when we visited. | was | The sentence is in the past.
es | PASS | A new bridge [will] built next year. | will be | A passive needs "be".
es | PASS | My car [has] stolen last night. | was | "Last night" calls for the past simple passive.
es | PASS | The report [must] finish by Friday. | must be | A passive needs "be".
es | CONF | He gave me some good [advices] about my career. | advice | "Advice" is uncountable.
es | CONF | I need to [borrow] you some money. | lend | You "lend" to others and "borrow" from them.
es | CONF | She [lay] the table before dinner. | laid | "Lay" means to put something down.
es | PHR | The plane [took] of an hour late. | off | "Take off" is the phrasal verb.
es | E | I have [many] luggage to carry. | much | "Luggage" is uncountable.
es | E | We have [less] books than the library. | fewer | "Fewer" is used with countable nouns.
es | S | The [peoples] in this town are very friendly. | people | "People" is already plural.
es | S | Pollution [are] a serious problem in big cities. | is | "Pollution" is uncountable.
es | S | The number of cars [are] increasing every year. | is | "The number of" takes a singular verb.
es | S | A lot of [informations] is available online. | information | "Information" is uncountable.
