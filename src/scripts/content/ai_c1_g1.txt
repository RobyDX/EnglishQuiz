# C1 grammar batch 1: tenses, modals, articles, pronouns, prepositions
mc | VT | The company ___ losses for three consecutive years before it was rescued by the government. | had been making | has been making :: The present perfect continuous does not fit a past rescue. | was making :: The past continuous does not show duration before the rescue. | made :: The simple past does not show duration. | The past perfect continuous shows duration before a past event.
mc | VT | At the time of the earthquake, most residents ___ asleep. | were | had been :: The past perfect would imply an earlier state. | have been :: The present perfect does not fit past events. | are :: The sentence is in the past. | The past simple of "be" describes the situation at the time.
mc | VT | By the time the new bridge opens, work on it ___ for nearly six years. | will have been going on | will go on :: The future perfect continuous shows duration. | goes on :: A present tense does not fit. | has gone on :: The present perfect does not fit a future time. | The future perfect continuous shows duration up to a future moment.
mc | VT | What ___ you ___ when I rang? | were / doing | did / do :: The past continuous shows an action in progress. | have / done :: The present perfect does not fit. | are / doing :: The sentence is in the past. | The past continuous shows an action in progress at a past moment.
mc | VT | If I ___ you, I would think very carefully before accepting the offer. | were | am :: The second conditional needs a past form. | was :: "Were" is the standard form in this advice structure. | would be :: "Would" is not used in the if-clause. | "If I were you" is a fixed advice structure.
mc | VT | The figures ___ since the last quarter and now show a steady recovery. | have been rising | rise :: The present simple does not show duration. | are rising :: The present continuous does not show duration since a point. | rose :: The simple past does not link to the present. | The present perfect continuous links change to now.
mc | VT | It was only when I read the letter again ___ I understood what she had meant. | that | when :: "When" duplicates the earlier "when". | which :: "Which" is ungrammatical here. | what :: "What" is ungrammatical here. | "It was only when ... that" is a cleft structure.
mc | VT | He is said to ___ the first person to cross the desert on foot. | have been | be being :: A continuous form changes the meaning. | had been :: A finite form is not used after "to". | being :: After "said to" an infinitive is needed. | "To have been" refers to an earlier event in a reporting structure.
mc | MOD | You ___ have told me earlier that you were coming; I would have prepared a proper meal. | could | needn't :: "Needn't have" says telling me was unnecessary. | mustn't :: "Mustn't have" is not used for criticism. | can't :: "Can't have" is a negative deduction. | "Could have" can express a reproach about a missed opportunity.
mc | MOD | I'm sure she ___ the phone ringing; she was in the garden. | can't have heard | mustn't have heard :: "Mustn't have" is not used for deductions. | couldn't hear :: This does not express a deduction about the past. | needn't have heard :: "Needn't have" says something was unnecessary. | "Can't have" + participle expresses a negative deduction.
mc | MOD | She ___ have been more than twenty when she wrote her first novel. | can't | needn't :: "Needn't have been" says it was unnecessary. | mustn't :: "Mustn't have" is not used for deductions. | shouldn't :: "Shouldn't have" expresses criticism. | "Can't have" expresses a negative deduction.
mc | MOD | We ___ well be late, so please start without us if we haven't arrived. | may | will to :: "Will" is not followed by "to". | shall :: "Shall well be" is not idiomatic. | ought :: "Ought well be" lacks "to". | "May well" expresses a strong possibility.
mc | ART | The film received ___ critical acclaim and several awards. | considerable | considerate :: "Considerate" means thoughtful. | considerably :: An adverb cannot modify a noun. | consideration :: "Consideration" is a noun. | "Considerable" means large in amount.
mc | ART | I would like to make ___ few suggestions before the meeting ends. | a | the :: "The few" has a different meaning. | one :: "One few" is ungrammatical. | – :: "A few" requires the article. | "A few" means some.
mc | ART | The new rules apply to ___ employees, regardless of their position. | all | every :: "Every employees" is ungrammatical. | each :: "Each employees" is ungrammatical. | whole :: "Whole employees" is ungrammatical. | "All" goes with plural nouns.
mc | PRON | Anyone ___ wishes to attend should register in advance. | who | whom :: "Whom" is an object form. | which :: "Which" refers to things. | whose :: "Whose" shows possession. | "Who" is the subject.
mc | PRON | The delegates, ___ were tired after the long journey, were shown to their rooms. | most of whom | most of them :: "Them" cannot join the clauses. | most of who :: After "of" the object form is needed. | most of which :: "Which" refers to things. | "Most of whom" is a standard relative construction.
mc | PRON | He has two brothers, ___ is a doctor. | one of whom | one of them :: "Them" cannot join the clauses. | one of who :: After "of" the object form is needed. | one of which :: "Which" refers to things. | "One of whom" joins the clauses.
mc | PREP | The company is currently ___ negotiations with a potential buyer. | in | on :: "On negotiations" is not standard. | at :: "At negotiations" is not standard. | with :: "With negotiations" is not standard. | "In negotiations" is the fixed collocation.
mc | PREP | The minister was forced to resign ___ the pressure of public opinion. | under | in :: "In the pressure" is not standard. | at :: "At the pressure" is not standard. | with :: "With the pressure" is not the idiom. | "Under pressure" is the fixed collocation.
mc | PREP | The film is set ___ the backdrop of the Spanish Civil War. | against | on :: "On the backdrop" is not standard. | at :: "At the backdrop" is not standard. | in :: "In the backdrop" is not standard. | "Set against the backdrop of" is the fixed phrase.
fb | VT | Scarcely had she sat down ___ the telephone rang. | when | than :: "Scarcely" is followed by "when". | as :: "As" does not complete the pair. | that :: "That" does not complete the pair. | "Scarcely ... when" is a correlative pair.
fb | VT | No sooner had the plane taken off ___ the storm struck. | than | when :: "No sooner" is followed by "than". | that :: "That" does not complete the pair. | as :: "As" does not complete the pair. | "No sooner ... than" is a correlative pair.
fb | VT | Never before ___ the city experienced such severe flooding. | had | has :: The past perfect fits the past narrative. | did :: "Did" is followed by the base form. | was :: "Was the city experienced" is ungrammatical. | "Never before had" is an inverted structure.
fb | VT | Not only ___ the proposal unrealistic, it was also unaffordable. | was | is :: The sentence is in the past. | were :: "The proposal" is singular. | did :: "Did the proposal unrealistic" is ungrammatical. | "Not only" at the start requires inversion.
fb | VT | Only after the results were published ___ the scale of the problem become clear. | did | had :: "Did" is needed in the inversion. | was :: "Was the scale become" is ungrammatical. | does :: The sentence is in the past. | "Only after" requires inversion.
fb | VT | Little ___ she realise that her life was about to change for ever. | did | does :: The sentence is in the past. | had :: "Had she realise" is ungrammatical. | was :: "Was she realise" is ungrammatical. | "Little did" is an inverted structure.
fb | MOD | I ___ rather you didn't mention it to anybody else for the moment. | would | had :: "Had rather" is dated. | should :: "Should rather" is rare. | might :: "Might rather" is not used. | "I would rather" is the standard form.
fb | ART | ___ of the two options is entirely satisfactory. | Neither | None :: "None" is for three or more. | No :: "No of" is ungrammatical. | Either :: "Either" would suggest at least one is. | "Neither" refers to two and is negative.
fb | PRON | The firm ___ I work is one of the largest in the region. | where | which :: "Which" needs a preposition here. | whose :: "Whose" shows possession. | that :: "That I work" needs a preposition. | "Where" follows a noun of place.
fb | PRON | It was the manager himself ___ announced the redundancies. | who | whom :: "Whom" is an object form. | which :: "Which" refers to things. | whose :: "Whose" shows possession. | "Who" is the subject of the cleft clause.
fb | PREP | She spoke ___ passion about the need for reform. | with | in :: "In passion" is not the collocation. | by :: "By passion" is not the collocation. | of :: "Of passion" is not the collocation. | "With passion" is the standard collocation.
fb | PREP | He was dismissed ___ misconduct after a lengthy inquiry. | for | of :: "Dismissed of" is not standard. | with :: "Dismissed with" is not standard. | by :: "Dismissed by" is not standard. | "Dismissed for" introduces the reason.
vf | VT | I ___ the report for hours and still have not finished. | have been writing | write :: The present simple does not show duration. | am writing :: The present continuous does not show duration. | wrote :: The simple past does not link to now. | The present perfect continuous links duration to now.
vf | VT | By the end of the month, the two companies ___ completely. | will have merged | will merge :: The future perfect shows completion. | merge :: A present tense does not fit. | have merged :: The present perfect does not fit. | "By the end of the month" calls for the future perfect.
vf | MOD | She ___ to have been the most talented student of her year. | is said | says :: The passive reporting structure is needed. | is saying :: A continuous form changes the meaning. | has said :: The subject receives the reporting. | "Be said to have been" is a reporting structure.
vf | ART | I have never had ___ objection to your plan. | any | some :: "Some" is not used in negatives like this. | no :: "Never ... no" is a double negative. | a few :: "Objection" is singular. | "Any" is used in negatives.
vc | VT | I ___ (think) about changing my job for some time now. | have been thinking | think :: The present simple does not show duration. | am thinking :: The present continuous does not show duration. | thought :: The simple past does not link to now. | The present perfect continuous links duration to now.
vc | MOD | It's high time the authorities ___ (address) the housing shortage. | addressed | address :: After "it's high time" a past form is used. | will address :: "Will" is not used here. | have addressed :: The present perfect does not fit. | "High time" + past simple states that action is overdue.
vc | PRON | She is a woman ___ (on / I / rely) in a crisis. | on whom I rely | whom I rely on her :: The pronoun is repeated. | on who I rely :: After a preposition use "whom". | which I rely :: "Which" refers to things. | "On whom I rely" is the formal pattern.
es | MOD | You [ought] have called to say that you would be late. | ought to | "Ought" needs "to" before "have".
es | ART | There is [less] people in the office today. | fewer | "Fewer" goes with countable nouns.
es | PRON | The manager [which] resigned yesterday was widely respected. | who | "Who" refers to people.
es | PREP | They were not aware [from] the dangers involved. | of | "Aware of" is the fixed collocation.
tr | VT | It is a long time since I last visited the town. | HAVEN'T | I ___ the town for a long time. | haven't visited | The present perfect with "for".
tr | MOD | It is possible that the letter has been lost in the post. | MAY | The letter ___ lost in the post. | may have been | "May have been" expresses a possibility.
tr | ART | I have no money at all left. | NOT | I ___ money left. | do not have any | "Not any" is the neutral negative.
tr | PRON | I will take whichever seat is available. | ANY | I will take ___ available. | any seat that is | "Any ... that" expresses free choice.
tr | PREP | The decision is a matter of urgency. | ISSUE | The decision is ___ urgency. | an issue of | A different noun with the same meaning.
